Series 03 — Risk & classification

Credit Card Fraud Detection

On a dataset where fraud is well under 1%, what does "accurate" even mean?

Preprocessed 1,000,000+ transaction records in Python, explored the fraud signal, and built classification models — judged on precision, recall, F1 and ROC‑AUC rather than raw accuracy, because on imbalanced data accuracy flatters a model that catches nothing.

Records 1M+ Type Self-directed Focus Imbalanced data
  • Scale handled in Pandas — cleaning and preprocessing a million rows without breaking the pipeline.
  • EDA before modelling to find the transaction patterns and risk factors worth feeding a model.
  • Honest evaluation — precision, recall, F1 and ROC‑AUC, with the precision/recall trade-off treated as a business call.
  • Feature influence ranked, so the result is explainable to a risk team, not a black box.
